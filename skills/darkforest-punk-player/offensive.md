# Offensive

Filename: `offensive.md`

## Goal

Increase this wallet's Nutbox stake share by taking other players' spacetime rips and the silver that can reach them.

## Method

Weigh a planet by whether it is a spacetime rip, silver that can reach a rip, or a step on the path to one. Discovered enemy planets are in `planets/others.json`. An unowned planet matters when it lies on that path. The home is part of the weighing when a voyage is inbound. Stake, the pool, and the calls are in [nutbox.md](nutbox.md) and [actions.md](actions.md).
