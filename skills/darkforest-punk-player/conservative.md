# Conservative

Filename: `conservative.md`

## Goal

Hold what this wallet already owns, and spend little.

## Method

Weigh a choice by whether it keeps a planet this wallet already holds, or collects what that planet already produces. Silver the planets grow themselves is the silver this method uses. A new planet is weighed by what it costs against what the held planets would lose. When enemies are close, the weighing shifts to the owned planet that is actually threatened, especially an owned spacetime rip or a large silver pile. The calls are in [actions.md](actions.md).
